05 / Process

Four steps.
One clear goal.

Good copy starts before the first sentence. I get clear on the audience, offer and action before I write.

Understand

Product, audience, offer, competitors and business goal.

02 — DISTILL

Position

Find the strongest promise and the language customers care about.

03 — DRAFT

Write

Hooks, structure, benefits, objections and a clear CTA.
